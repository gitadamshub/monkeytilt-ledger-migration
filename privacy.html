Privacy policy

Effective October 24, 2026. This policy covers the standalone MonkeyTilt Ledger Migration connector and these informational pages. The operator is Tilt Rips LLC, Nevada, USA. Contact adam@tiltrips.com.

Purpose and access

The connector is for internal accounting migration and reconciliation. An authorized administrator must approve each QuickBooks connection. It reads only authorized company files. Accounting records may include company details, accounts, balances, transactions, vendor and customer names, and business contact information.

Use of information

Information is used to maintain, reconcile, and report the authorized company's books. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or train models with it. The current release does not post accounting entries or move money.

Where information goes

The connector communicates with Intuit's documented authentication and accounting APIs. Retrieved accounting records are written to private local files on the authorized operator's computer. They are not sent to this public website. The connector itself does not send records to AI providers.

Separately, authorized finance personnel use company-approved tools, including Codex and Claude, outside this connector. That wider workflow may process accounting information under the company's arrangements with those providers. This statement does not describe the wider workflow as AI-free.

Authentication and safeguards

Intuit handles authorization and account sign-in. The connector does not ask for or store an Intuit account password. App secrets and authorization tokens are stored in macOS Keychain. Local accounting files reside on a FileVault-encrypted Mac and are written with private file permissions. API traffic uses verified HTTPS. Connections are restricted to an explicit company allowlist.

The authorization callback is handled locally or over a private HTTPS connection to the operator's computer, not by this website. The callback validates the request state and the expected company, does not log authorization codes, and redirects to an empty completion response.

Retention and disconnection

Accounting exports may be retained for accounting, audit, tax, and legal requirements. A successful local disconnect command revokes Intuit authorization and then removes the stored tokens from this Mac. If Intuit reports an error, local tokens are retained until the result is resolved or the operator explicitly removes them. Disconnecting in QuickBooks does not erase this Mac's Keychain entries. The operator can use the separately confirmed local forget command to remove those entries; that command does not revoke or verify remote access. Neither command deletes QuickBooks records or accounting evidence. Requests concerning retained business records can be sent to the contact above.

This website

These static pages have no sign-up forms, analytics scripts, advertising, or cookies set by us. GitHub Pages hosts them and may process ordinary web-request information under GitHub's privacy statement. Do not enter credentials or accounting information on these pages.

Changes

We will update this policy if the connector's handling of information changes. No security certification or independent security audit is claimed.
