MonkeyTilt Ledger Migration

An internal accounting connector operated by Tilt Rips LLC.

The connector runs locally on an authorized operator's Mac. It connects only to QuickBooks company files that an authorized administrator approves. It is not offered for public sign-up and is not a payment service.

Current release

The current connector handles authorization and accounting retrieval. An explicitly approved sandbox-only pilot tests small journals and matching reversals. Posting to live company books is not enabled in this release. Any later production posting capability requires separate controls and approval.

AI boundary

The connector is deterministic software. It does not call AI services or contain a model. Our wider bookkeeping workflow uses Codex and Claude outside the connector to assist with preparation and review.

This website contains information only. It does not receive accounting records, collect login credentials, or process authorization callbacks.
