Connect or reconnect

For authorized internal operators only. This page does not collect credentials or connect a company by itself.

  1. Use the locally installed connector and its approved configuration.
  2. Start its connect command for the exact approved company identifier.
  3. Complete Intuit's authorization screen in Work Chrome. Check the company name before consenting.
  4. The connector validates the callback and stores tokens in macOS Keychain.

If authorization expires or is revoked, repeat the same process for the same approved company. Never paste passwords, app secrets, or authorization codes into this website or a support email.
