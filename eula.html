Ending access

An authorized administrator may disconnect the connector at any time. A successful local disconnect command revokes access and removes this Mac's stored tokens. Disconnecting in QuickBooks requires separate local token cleanup. If remote revocation fails, the operator can explicitly remove local tokens with the forget command, which does not confirm or perform remote revocation. Accounting records and evidence are retained.
